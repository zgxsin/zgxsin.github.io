Hiking, Day Trip | Lauterbrunnen, Wengen, Männlichen | 10 October 2021

We started from Zurich HB to Wengen. Then we took the cable car from Wengen to Männlichen. Afterwards, we hiked back to Wengen, passing by Kleine Scheidegg.
