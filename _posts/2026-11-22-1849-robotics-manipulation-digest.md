## Academia

- **Video Friday: Drone Squeezes Itself to Fit Through Tight Spaces** (Nov 20). Researchers demonstrated TeCoBots, soft snake-like robots that use the same claw end effector to grasp objects and link onto each other without extra docking hardware. Dual-purpose claws let modular soft robots transition between single-unit manipulation, such as lifting a 2.5-liter water bottle, and multi-robot configurations for tasks beyond one unit's reach.[^ref-1]

## Industry

- **Can Robots Bring Stroke Surgery to Remote Hospitals?** (Nov 16). Remedy Robotics demonstrated its Remedy N1 robotic catheter system, allowing an operator to teleoperate a flexible catheter via an Xbox controller and foot pedal under live X-ray guidance. Remote teleoperation of flexible endovascular tools aims to bring time-critical ischemic stroke thrombectomies to hospitals lacking on-site neurointerventionalists.[^ref-2]

## Application Readiness

- **Video Friday: Drone Squeezes Itself to Fit Through Tight Spaces**[^ref-1]
  - **Readiness** (Research prototype). For modular grasping and self-assembly, the evidence consists of lab hardware demonstrations showing a single unit climbing, rolling, and lifting a 2.5-liter water bottle, as well as linking into chains and loops.
  - **Pros.** Eliminates dedicated docking hardware by reusing the object-grasping claw for inter-robot connection.
  - **Cons.** Payload capacity, cycle time, and reliability across varied industrial or unstructured objects remain unreported.
- **Can Robots Bring Stroke Surgery to Remote Hospitals?**[^ref-2]
  - **Readiness** (Research prototype). For remote endovascular thrombectomy, the system was demonstrated in an office setting using a silicone-tubing vascular phantom under X-ray rather than in clinical trials.
  - **Pros.** Uses a familiar game-controller interface to steer a thin, flexible catheter through branching vessel paths.
  - **Cons.** Clinical safety, latency tolerance over long-distance networks, and performance in live human vasculature are not yet established.
