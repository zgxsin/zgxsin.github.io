## Academia

- **Ferrofluid Shape Control with Deep Reinforcement Learning** (Nov 20). A new arXiv preprint reports using deep model-based reinforcement learning to control transient ferrofluid shapes with a single fixed electromagnet and visual observations. Demonstrating reliable fluid shaping through physical interaction could expand actuation options for soft robots operating in confined spaces. [arXiv Robotics](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25232>)
- **Learning Ankle Exoskeleton Control for Reactive Standing Balance Through Neuromusculoskeletal Simulation** (Nov 20). An arXiv preprint details a reinforcement-learning controller deployed on a bilateral ankle exoskeleton that uses only inertial sensor measurements to increase tolerated standing perturbations by 11.8% in five healthy subjects. Reducing reliance on explicit perturbation detection and training via virtual human models could simplify wearable balance-assistance hardware for older adults. [arXiv Robotics](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25480>)
